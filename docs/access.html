SECURITY GOVERNANCE

CIA triad & shared access

A safe university portal protects confidentiality, integrity, and availability on every request.

Confidentiality

Only authorized people see information. Enforce SSO, MFA, role-based access, encrypted HTTPS sessions, and FERPA-aware data minimization.

Least privilege

Integrity

Keep grades, enrollments, and identities correct. Use server-side authorization, validation, audit logs, approvals, and immutable change history.

Verified changes

Availability

Keep the portal reachable with backups, monitoring, rate limiting, disaster recovery, and tested incident response.

Resilient service
